Onsala Space Observatory's Tech Talks

  • When: Mondays 10-11 am
  • Where: Onsala Space Observatory, Seminar room
  • Contact: TechTalk mailing List

The idea for this is to be an informal meeting where we can discuss tools/software packages/programming languages/general computing issues that we use and encounter on a daily basis. The overall goal of this is to learn from each other and help everyone to spend less time on technicalities that others have already mastered.
