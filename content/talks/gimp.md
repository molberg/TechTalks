## What is it?
### Wikipedia

> (GNU Image Manipulation Program) is a free and open-source raster
> graphics editor used for image retouching and editing,
> free-form drawing, converting between different image formats,
> and more specialized tasks.

## Gimp - what I use it for
 * Enhance quality of and/or modify plots taken from papers for talks
 * Create schematics (i.e. free drawing)
 * scale/retouch images and photos

## Get it, docs, manuals
* linux distributions like Ubuntu ship it as a package (get it via apt-get)
