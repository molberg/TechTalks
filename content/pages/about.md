## Onsala Space Observatory's Tech Talks

 * When:        **Mondays 10-11 am**
 * Where:       [Onsala Space Observatory](https://www.google.se/maps/place/Onsala+rymdobservatorium,+Chalmers/@57.3961849,11.9082682,14z), Seminar room
 * Contact:     [TechTalk  mailing List](mailto:oso-techtalk.see@chalmers.se)

The idea for this is to be an informal meeting where we can discuss
tools/software packages/programming languages/general computing issues
that we use and encounter on a daily basis. The overall goal of this
is to learn from each other and help everyone to spend less time on
technicalities that others have already mastered.
